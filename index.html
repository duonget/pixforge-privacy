PixForge Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers the PixForge Studio desktop app for Windows (Microsoft Store) and the PixForge Studio - Image Converter extension for Microsoft Edge.

Your images stay on your device

PixForge Studio converts, compresses, resizes and watermarks images entirely on your device. Your images, their file names and their metadata are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Converted files are written next to your originals or to a folder you choose, and the originals are not changed. The File Explorer right-click menu, "Open with" and "Share" only hand the selected file paths to the app on the same PC.

What we collect

Nothing. PixForge Studio has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive data from the app.

What is stored on your device

The app remembers your settings on your device so it works the same way next time: your language, light or dark theme, conversion settings (including the output folder and watermark logo you chose), the presets you saved, whether you have already opened the rating dialog (so the app stops suggesting it), and a cached copy of your Pro license status. Images you paste into the app (Ctrl+V) are kept in the app's own cache folder so they can be converted, and are deleted after 7 days. The app reads the clipboard only when you paste. If you turn on a watch folder, the app remembers that folder and, while it runs, converts the new images that appear in it; it looks at no other folder. You can remove all of this by uninstalling the app or the extension.

Diagnostic info

"Copy diagnostic info" (the ⓘ button) puts a short text on your clipboard: the app and Windows versions, your conversion settings and the errors of the current batch, including the names of the files that failed. Nothing is sent anywhere; you decide whether to paste it into a message to us.

Microsoft Store

Purchases of Pro (Lifetime Pro or the Monthly Pass), license checks and the in-app rating dialog are handled by the Microsoft Store on your device. The app only asks the Store which Pro add-ons are active and what they cost. Payment details and your Microsoft account are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; we do not receive them. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Microsoft account.

Edge extension

When you right-click an image on a web page and choose "Save image as JPG" or "Save image as PNG", the extension downloads that image from the address the page already uses, converts it in your browser and saves it with Edge's download manager. Images you open in the extension's popup or side panel are converted in the browser the same way. Nothing is uploaded.

Permissions: contextMenus adds the right-click items; activeTab lets the extension read the image you clicked on the current page; downloads saves the converted file; sidePanel shows the converter in Edge's side panel. If the image is stored on another site (for example an image CDN), the extension asks you first, and only for that site; you can withdraw this in Edge's extension settings. The extension does not read or send your browsing history or the content of web pages.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
